About

I am a final-year medical student at King’s College London with academic interests in surgical research, surgical education, robotic surgery, and technology-enabled assessment. My current work focuses on objective evaluation of robotic surgical skill, motion-derived performance metrics, simulation-based training, and evidence synthesis.

I hope to pursue a career in academic surgery, combining clinical practice with research that improves the quality, objectivity, and accessibility of surgical training. I am particularly interested in work that translates methodological rigour and emerging technology into meaningful improvements in education, workforce sustainability, and patient care.

Research Interests

  • Robotic surgical skill acquisition and learning curves
  • Objective assessment of technical performance
  • Simulation-based and cross-platform surgical training
  • Motion tracking and multimodal performance analytics
  • Machine learning in surgical assessment
  • Surgeon wellbeing and workforce sustainability
  • Systematic reviews and meta-analysis
